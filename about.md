I'm **Tung Lin** — a data analyst and transportation enthusiast based between Taipei, Los Angeles, and the San Francisco Bay Area.

I specialize in transit planning, data visualization, and web development. I'm drawn to tools and services that serve all communities, and I approach every project with full deliberate commitment.

## Background

I studied at UCLA, where I balanced coursework with roles at the Daily Bruin — working up from Video Intern to Assistant Video Producer, Sports Intern, and Online Intern. Along the way I also worked as an EMT-B for over a year and volunteered at Silicon Valley Bike Exchange, repairing bikes and staffing community events.

Professionally, I've focused on transportation data. Most of my recent work is with the **Parking Reform Network**, where I've built and maintained their full-stack parking reform map, managed a new parking code database, and driven data and UX improvements across their tools.

- **Data & GIS:** QGIS, Leaflet, Pandas, GeoPandas
- **Web:** HTML, CSS, Jekyll, Parcel, GitHub Actions
- **Interests:** Transit policy, open data, sustainable cities
